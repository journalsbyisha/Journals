The Beginning of the Silent Feminist of This Age
Hi. First of all, thank you for visiting this space where I want to share my point of view on different things, mainly as a form of emotional release. This is not about any revolution, nor does it stand behind any particular revolutionary movement. However, the opportunity through which I am able to express all my bhaang bhosda and frustration has been made possible by the revolutionary idea of feminism.
In today’s article, I want to share my thoughts on having a radical dissent from mainstream ideas while still surviving within a socio-economic structure where you are constantly exposed to experiences that challenge and mutilate the very thoughts upon which you have built your existence and reality.
Let us begin with the immediate environment around us — home. A home should feel like a safe boundary where you are allowed to be vulnerable, where you can reset yourself for the ruthless outside world. But in many of our experiences, we have seen that the very people who are supposed to be the pillars of the home become the enemies of your psychological existence.
Parents who always wanted a son for their social security. Brothers who are so privileged within their delusional victimhood that they deny the very existence of the problem. Forget addressing the issue — they remain in complete denial that anything is even wrong around us.
Let me be clear: these people are biologically alive and socially active when it comes to matters involving their own scrutiny or comfort. But for the rest of the world, even if the least secure member of their own family is suffering, they often remain indifferent.
In such a situation, what can you do?
First, become aware of your reality.My reality is that I am a girl living in a hyper-patriarchal and insecure society where the social structure is deeply corrupt, and where the systems of justice often feel broken, polarized, and discriminatory in nature. Reality often reveals itself through discrimination and the absence of justice in your favor.
Allow these harsh truths to settle within you. Once you accept reality, only then can you move forward toward the next step. I know this sounds brutal, but it is a perpetually recurring phenomenon.
Second, build your inner strength.Internally, you must develop a strong philosophy for your life. You need to create a space within yourself that allows your soul to remain alive, youthful, joyful, and aware.I know that facing darkness while trying to nurture a beautiful inner garden may seem contradictory, but that very balance is what keeps you conscious, capable, and alive at the same time.
So work on your mental space. Work on the philosophy of your life.At the same time, strengthen your physical existence as well.Invest in your muscles and your spine — not only literally through exercise and an active lifestyle, but symbolically through education, real-life skills, and academic qualifications. These things support you during life’s unpredictable struggles.
Work on your overall interpersonal skills and your ability to interact confidently in the real world.
Lastly, wherever possible, express your thoughts. It could be through platforms like X or through your personal journal.Thoughts that are shaped through suffering and resistance do not necessarily need an audience. They need your attention, your commitment, and your honesty — especially when you do not have support from your surroundings.You do not always need to become a lone tree standing against an extreme storm.
Your existence matters.You matter.

We will stay connected through this written form, until the next radical thought.Thank you for being here and for paying attention.
